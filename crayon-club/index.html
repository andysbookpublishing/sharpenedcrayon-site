Questions

Is Crayon Club a physical product?

No. Crayon Club is a digital PDF you print at home, as many times as you like. The Party Pack is the printed option.

Do I need to send a photo?

No, it's optional. A photo helps us match the look. A parent or guardian uploads it, we use it only to draw the line-art character, and we delete it 30 days after delivery.

Can you include our business or school?

Yes. Share your website and we'll use your own logo or storefront, once you confirm you own the images or have the rights to them.

When do Club books arrive?

On the 1st of each month. Your first book arrives within 48 hours of approving the character.

How fast is a Party Pack?

A digital proof within 48 hours, two revision rounds, then standard delivery in 7-10 business days after you approve, or 4-6 business days with rush (+$75). Order at least 2 weeks before your event.

Can I cancel Crayon Club?

Yes, anytime online. You keep access through the period you paid for.

When do I pay?

After we confirm your order details by email. Submitting the form reserves your spot; it doesn't charge you.
