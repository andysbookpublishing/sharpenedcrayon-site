Why a custom book?

Not another logo on a stock book.

Most branded kids’ giveaways are generic books with a logo stamped on the cover. Ours are drawn around your world: your building, your people, your town. That makes them something families hold on to and talk about.

  • Original illustrations. No clip art, no trademarked characters.
  • Your brand, done tastefully. Logo, colors and contact details, plus a reason to come back.
  • Print-ready and printable. Files for a commercial printer plus a version for your office printer.
  • Printed books included, shipped anywhere in the US. Every package includes printed books and standard shipping to one US address.

How it works

  1. Free sample.

    Tell us about your business. We’ll sketch a free 3-page personalized sample.

  2. Pick a package.

    Choose your book; printed books are included. A 50% deposit gets us started.

  3. We create, you review.

    We build original pages around your brand, with revision rounds to get it right.

  4. Print & deliver.

    Approve the proof and pay the balance. We print your books and ship them anywhere in the US.
