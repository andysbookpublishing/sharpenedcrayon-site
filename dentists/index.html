Why custom?

Not another tooth book with a logo stamped on it.

Most dental coloring books for kids are stock titles: the same pages every practice hands out, with a 1-color logo on the cover. Ours are custom dental activity books drawn around your practice. Kids color your waiting room, meet your team and follow your mascot through brushing, flossing and healthy snacks. Your name is on the pages they actually look at, not just the cover.

  • Your office and team, drawn as original line art from photos you send
  • An original mascot, or your existing one, as the star of the book
  • Your logo and contact details on the back cover, so parents know who to call
  • English and Spanish editions available

Dental office questions

Can the book feature our real office and team?

Yes. We draw your building, your team and an original mascot from photos you send, and your logo and contact details go on the back cover.

Is the content dentally accurate?

We write simple, kid-level habits (brushing twice a day, flossing, healthy snacks) and you review and approve every page before printing, so your team signs off on the messaging.

Can we get it in English and Spanish?

Yes. A bilingual edition is $250 on Starter, $600 on Signature and included in Premium.

How many books should a practice order?

Packages include 250, 500 or 1,000 printed books. A simple way to size it: a year of new pediatric patients plus any school visits. Extra copies are $300 per 100 on the same print run ($400 per 100 on Premium).

What ages are these for?

Books aimed at children ages 3 and under need a compliance review before we can quote them, so tell us your youngest patients’ ages up front.
