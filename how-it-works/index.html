How it works

From first chat to a box of books.

Four steps, clear dates and no surprises on price. We design remotely, so your business can be anywhere in the US.

  1. Free sample.

    Tell us about your business. We’ll sketch a free 3-page personalized sample, with no obligation. We reply to every request within 1 business day.

  2. Pick a package.

    Choose your book (printed books are included), and we’ll agree a short brief: what your book features, who it’s for, languages and your deadline. A 50% deposit gets us started.

  3. We create, you review.

    We build original pages around your brand. You send one consolidated list of changes per revision round: Starter includes 1 round, Signature 2 and Premium 3.

  4. Print & deliver.

    Approve the proof and pay the balance. We print your books and ship them anywhere in the US, with standard shipping to one address included.

What we’ll ask you for

  • Your logo (PNG, JPG, PDF or vector files) and brand colors
  • Photos of your storefront, team, mascot or neighborhood (only images you have the rights to)
  • Contact details and any offer for the back page
  • The age group and any second language
  • Your quantity and in-hand date

Simple money terms

  • 50% deposit on the package price to start
  • Balance due at proof approval, before printing
  • Pay by card or US bank (ACH)
  • Printed books and standard shipping included
  • Invoices due within 7 days
