A holiday gift your clients’ kids will actually open

Mugs get re-gifted and gift baskets are gone by Friday. A custom coloring book starring your company, your team and your mascot goes home, gets colored and ends up on the fridge. It’s one of the few unique corporate holiday gifts the whole family uses. We design remotely and ship printed books anywhere in the US.

What it works out to per book

Shopping for corporate holiday gifts “under $10”? Every package is all-in: design, printed books and standard shipping to one US address.

Bigger team or client list? That’s where employee holiday gifts in bulk get easy: extra copies are $300 per 100 on Starter and Signature ($400 per 100 on Premium), added to the same print run. Orders over 1,000 copies are quoted.

Per-book figures are the package price divided by the books included. We confirm your exact total in your quote. Tax is extra where it applies.

  • Starter: $1,450 for 12 pages and 250 books, about $5.80 a book.
  • Signature: $4,250 for 24 to 32 pages and 500 books, about $8.50 a book.
  • Premium: from $8,000 for 40+ pages and 1,000 books, from about $8 a book, with a bilingual edition, custom mascot and matching printables included.

Corporate gift questions

What’s the holiday deadline?

Holiday orders for December delivery: order by Thu, Oct 22 for guaranteed holiday delivery. Orders Oct 23 to 30 are rush only (+$500, subject to availability). Design approval is due by Thu Nov 5, and the balance is due at proof approval (by Mon Nov 9 at the latest, subject to printer lead time).

Can we ship to many addresses?

Standard shipping to one address in the contiguous US is included. Extra addresses, expedited shipping, Alaska/Hawaii and international delivery are quoted.

Can our company mascot or product appear?

Yes. We can draw your mascot, office, team and products, or create an original mascot for you.

Is it appropriate for clients without kids?

It works best for audiences with families. Send it to employees’ families and client contacts who have kids, alongside your regular gift.

Do you offer net terms for larger companies?

Net-30 terms are available to enterprise clients after a credit check.
