Prices and quotes

Prices are in USD and include printed books and standard shipping to one address in the contiguous US. Taxes are extra. A quote isn’t an order until you approve it and pay the deposit.

Project terms (summary)

  • 50% deposit on the package price to start; balance due before the print run is ordered or final files are released.
  • Invoices are due within 7 days.
  • Cancellation: before work starts, deposit refunded less a $100 admin fee; once work starts, we keep the 50% deposit and nothing more is due. Print orders can’t be canceled once placed.
  • Overs/unders of up to ±5% of the ordered print quantity are billed or credited at $3.00 a book.
  • You get a license to print, distribute and promote your book for your business, not a copyright assignment. You confirm you have the rights to the logos and images you provide.
  • The free 3-page sample has no obligation; the sample art stays ours unless you order.
