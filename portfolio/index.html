Sample project · Pediatric dentist · 12 pages

Brush, floss & smile!

Fictional sample client: Sparkle & Brush Pediatric Dental

A cheerful take-home book for young patients. Toby Tooth, an original mascot, shows kids how to brush 2 times a day, floss and pick healthy snacks, then visits the friendly (fictional) Sparkle & Brush office. Pages pair coloring scenes with simple activities: a find-the-toothbrush maze, a connect-the-dots tooth, a word search, a spot-the-difference page and a “Draw your smile!” page. The back cover carries the practice’s logo, contact details, Toby’s tooth tips and the answer keys.

Ideal for: new-patient welcome bags, February’s Children’s Dental Health Month, school visits.

Sample project · Real estate agent · 12 pages

Welcome to Maple Hollow!

Fictional sample client: Color Your Neighborhood: Maple Hollow

A “color your neighborhood” keepsake for a fictional agent’s closing gifts. Kids color the (fictional) neighborhood’s welcome sign, the park, the Main Street shops, a cozy home with a yard, and the library and community garden. There’s a moving-truck maze, a connect-the-dots house, a word search, a spot-the-difference page and a “Draw your home!” page, plus a back cover with the agent’s name, logo, contact details and a neighborhood fun list.

Ideal for: closing gifts, open-house giveaways, neighborhood mailers, community events.

Sample project · Family restaurant · 12 pages

Let’s eat & color!

Fictional sample client: Nonna Lucia’s Family Kitchen

A kids’ menu companion that keeps little ones busy until dinner arrives. Kids meet the menu crew (Pippa Pizza, Chef Mo Meatball, Spaghetti Sam and Gigi Gelato), color Nonna’s kitchen and a family dinner table, and play “Design your own pizza,” a find-the-spaghetti maze, a spot-the-difference page, a word search with menu items and a kids’ menu page with tic-tac-toe and an invent-a-dish challenge. The back cover invites families back with a “Kids eat with us” box for an offer.

Ideal for: kids’ menus, birthday parties, takeout bags, catering orders.

Sample project · Corporate holiday gift · 12 pages

Bitsy’s winter wonderland

Fictional sample client: Tinkerwren Software

A holiday gift book from a (fictional) software company to the families of its employees and clients. Bitsy, the company’s original helper-bot mascot, joins the office winter party, builds a snow bot outside the office and delivers a gift through a maze. Kids also design their own app, count winter lights, connect the dots to a rocket and color the team volunteering at a coat and food drive. The back page carries a warm holiday note from the team.

Ideal for: employee family gifts, client and partner gift boxes, holiday parties and open houses.

Penny’s winter savings
