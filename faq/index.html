FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find yours? Contact us or request a quote and ask away.

Packages, payment & timing

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. We’ll create a free 3-page personalized sample before you commit.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit on the design fee starts your project. The balance is due before final print-ready files are released or the print run is ordered. Printing is paid in full when you approve the printer quote. Invoices are due within 7 days. Net-30 terms are available only to promotional-products distributors and enterprise clients after a credit check.

What if I need to cancel?

Before work starts, we refund your deposit less a $100 admin fee. After work starts, the deposit is kept as a kill fee. After the first full draft, the full design fee is due and we deliver the files completed so far. Print orders can’t be canceled once placed with the printer. Projects on hold for more than 30 days move to our next open slot; after 90 days on hold, they’re treated as canceled.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated list of changes to a draft. Starter includes 1 round, Signature 2 and Premium 3. Extra rounds are $250. New concepts or extra pages are priced as add-ons.

Who owns the book?

You get a license to print, distribute and promote your book for your business. Full rights terms are in our service agreement. A small Sharpened Crayon credit line goes on the back page by default; it’s optional, your choice.

Can you use a famous cartoon character?

No. We only create original art, and we don’t use trademarked characters or third-party logos without written permission. Your own logo is welcome; you confirm you have the rights to it.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as one of our tools. We use digital design tools, including AI assistants, to help draft artwork, layouts and text. Our sample books were drawn as clean vector line art in code written with an AI assistant; no AI image generator was used for them. Every book is checked against our quality checklist and approved by you before printing. All text is typeset, never generated inside an image. Ask us and we’ll tell you where AI was used in your book.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 1 to 2 weeks, Signature 3 to 4 weeks and Premium 4 to 6 weeks. Rush is available for +25% when we have capacity. It starts once your brief is approved and the deposit is received. Printing and shipping add time; we’ll confirm dates in your quote.

What’s the holiday deadline?

Holiday orders for December delivery: order by Thu Oct 22 for guaranteed holiday delivery (Signature books need to start by Oct 15). Orders Oct 23 to 30 are rush only, +25%, subject to availability. Design approval is due by Thu Nov 5, and print quote approval and payment by Mon Nov 9 (subject to printer lead time).

Do you include crayons? Can you make books for toddlers?

We don’t bundle crayons or other art supplies with books. Books aimed at children ages 3 and under need a compliance review before we can quote them.

Books, printing & shipping

What size are the books?

Every book is 8.5 x 11 in (US Letter), with original black-line art designed for crayons and markers.

What paper do you print on? Will markers bleed through?

We recommend uncoated paper of at least 60 to 70 lb text weight for the inside pages, so crayons and markers grip without bleeding through. If your audience loves markers, we can print pages single-sided. The exact paper is confirmed in your written print quote.

Is there a minimum order?

There’s no minimum if you print the files yourself. The Starter + Print bundle includes 250 copies, typical print runs are 500 to 1,000, and we’ll quote small runs (around 100) on request.

Do you ship? How much does shipping cost?

Yes. We arrange the print run and ship the books to you. Shipping and taxes are extra and appear in your written print quote before anything is printed.

Can we reorder later?

Yes. We’ll quote separate reprints on request. Bigger runs cost less per copy, so it’s often worth ordering a little extra up front: extra copies are $300 per 100 when added to a run of 500+.

Can the book be bilingual?

Yes. A bilingual edition (one second language, e.g., English/Spanish) is $250 for Starter, $600 for Signature, and included in Premium.

Are the books in your portfolio real clients?

No. They’re sample projects we created to show our style. The businesses are fictional. We’ll only show real client work with the client’s written permission.
