About

Your brand, ready to color.

Sharpened Crayon designs original coloring and activity books starring your business, so kids have fun and families keep your name on the kitchen table for months. We handle the art, layout and printing.

Why “sharpened”?

The playful part, the crayon, is the product. The sharpened part is the promise: precise, polished, on-brand and on time. Proofs, deadlines, print specs and invoices done right.

Who’s behind it

Founded by Andy Lujan and based in Gilroy, CA. We design remotely and work with businesses across the US.

How we work

  • Original art only. No clip art, no trademarked characters, no third-party logos without written permission.
  • Built for crayons. Clean black lines, closed shapes and areas sized for the age group.
  • Checked page by page. Every book goes through our quality checklist: mazes solved, word searches checked, spelling and your contact details proofread.
  • Print-ready files. Proper bleed and safe margins for a commercial printer, plus a version for your office printer.
  • Honest about tools. Ask us anytime how your book was made. See our FAQ.
