Privacy Policy

Draft · Last updated October 12, 2026 (children’s section pending review)

This policy explains what information Sharpened Crayon (“we”) collects through sharpenedcrayon.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you email us, for example to request a quote, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send: your name, business details, project details, logo files or links, and any other information you include.

This website doesn’t use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your request, prepare quotes and samples, and deliver your project. We’ll only add you to a mailing list if you ask, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We share it only with services we use to run the business (such as our email provider, our payment processor and our printer for delivery details), or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as needed for the project and our business records. To see, correct or delete your information, email sharpenedcrayon@mail.grokbot.com.

Children, Crayon Club and Party Packs

Draft, pending review. Our services are for parents, guardians, businesses and other adults. Children don’t create accounts or send us information. For Crayon Club and Party Pack orders, a parent or guardian may give us a child’s first name, age range, interests and an optional photo. We don’t ask for last names, birthdays, schools or a child’s own email.

  • Kids’ photos may be uploaded only by a parent or legal guardian (18+). We use them only to draw the line-art character for that order, and we delete them 30 days after delivery (sooner if you ask).
  • Teacher, agent or business-owner photos: the uploader confirms the person pictured agreed. The same use limit and 30-day deletion apply.
  • Website images: if you give us a website, you confirm you own its images or have the rights to let us use them in your book.
  • No marketing use of a child’s name, photo or book (or any order) unless you opt in separately. We never sell this information.
  • Order details are kept only as long as needed to make and support your books, and deleted on request or 90 days after a membership ends. Email us to review, correct or delete them.
