Quick answers

What add-ons are there?

The common ones: extra pages are $300 per 4 pages, an extra revision round is $250, a custom mascot is $400, and a bilingual edition is $250 (Starter) or $600 (Signature). Need it faster? Rush is a flat +$500 on any package, subject to availability. Premium already includes the mascot, bilingual edition and printables.

How many revisions do I get?

Starter includes 1 round, Signature 2 and Premium 3. A round is one list of changes you send us. Extra rounds are $250.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 1 to 2 weeks, Signature 3 to 4 weeks and Premium 4 to 6 weeks, counted from your deposit and approved brief. Then printing takes about 2 weeks plus shipping. We confirm exact dates in your quote.

How do deposit and payment work?

50% of the package price to start. The rest is due before we order your print run. Printed books and standard shipping are already included in the price. Resellers: contact us.

What is the Kids’ Corner subscription?

Fresh activities with your brand every month. $500/month: 1 printable activity sheet a month, an 8-page mini-book each quarter and 4 social posts a month. $1,000/month (Plus): 2 sheets and 8 posts a month, a 12-page mini-book each quarter, plus bilingual versions. 3-month minimum, then cancel with 30 days’ notice.

What’s the holiday order date?

Order by Thu Oct 22 for guaranteed holiday delivery (Signature books need to start by Oct 15). Orders from Oct 23 to 30 are rush only (+$500), subject to availability.

How many books do I get?

Every package includes printed books delivered to you: 250 with Starter, 500 with Signature and 1,000 with Premium. Need more? Extra copies are $300 per 100 on Starter and Signature, and $400 per 100 on Premium, added to the same print run. Orders over 1,000 copies are quoted. Standard shipping to one US address is included; extra addresses or rush shipping are quoted. Tax is extra where it applies.
