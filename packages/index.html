Quick answers

What add-ons are there?

The common ones: extra pages are $300 per 4 pages, an extra revision round is $150, a custom mascot is $400, and a bilingual edition is $250 (Starter) or $600 (Signature). Need it faster? Rush is a flat +$500 on any package, subject to availability. Premium already includes the mascot, bilingual edition and printables.

How many revisions do I get?

Starter includes 2 rounds, Signature 4 and Premium 6. A round is one list of changes you send us. Extra rounds are $150.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 1 to 2 weeks, Signature 3 to 4 weeks and Premium 4 to 6 weeks, counted from your deposit and approved brief. Then printing takes about 2 weeks plus shipping. We confirm exact dates in your quote.

How do deposit and payment work?

A 50% deposit reserves your spot: Starter $725, Signature $2,125, Premium $4,000 (from), Founding $750. The balance is due at proof approval, before printing. Pay by card or US bank (ACH). Printed books and standard shipping are already included in the price. Resellers: contact us.

What’s the holiday order date?

Order by Thu, Oct 22 for guaranteed holiday delivery. Orders from Oct 23 to 30 are rush only (+$500), subject to availability.

How many books do I get?

Every package includes printed books delivered to you: 250 with Starter, 500 with Signature and 1,000 with Premium. Need more? Extra copies are $300 per 100 on Starter and Signature, and $400 per 100 on Premium, added to the same print run. Orders over 1,000 copies are quoted. Standard shipping to one US address is included; extra addresses or rush shipping are quoted. Tax is extra where it applies.
