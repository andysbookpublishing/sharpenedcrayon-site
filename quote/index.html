What to include

  • Business name, as you’d like it to appear in the book
  • Industry (dentist, real estate, restaurant, school, event, corporate gift…)
  • Quantity: 250, 500, 1,000 or more books
  • Deadline: when you need books in hand
  • Ship-to city and state: we ship anywhere in the US
  • Your logo: attach it or paste a link
  • Nice to have: what the book should feature (your office and team, mascot, neighborhood, menu, local landmarks), languages, age group, the package you’re interested in and a budget range
